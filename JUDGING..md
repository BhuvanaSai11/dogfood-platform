# Judging and Scoring Methodology

## Overview
The platform implements a structured workflow for evaluating submissions once the submission window closes.

## Scoring Strategy (T1-Core)
* **Role Separation:** Judges are assigned to specific tracks or projects without administrative privileges[cite: 10].
* **Scoring Criteria:** Judges evaluate submissions on a standardized numerical scale based on functionality, completeness, and adherence to requirements.
* **Integrity:** Prevents unauthorized score modifications and ensures isolated evaluation metrics per judge.
